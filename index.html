The three units at a glance

01 Discover: AI Strategy and Opportunity (AU0009)

Understand the AI landscape and make confident investment decisions. Explore AI opportunities, evaluate tools and suppliers with structured criteria, navigate procurement and toolstack design, and build AI-powered solutions using tools like Claude Code and Lovable.

Duration: 30 hours Topics: 5 Funding: Levy eligible Start: May 2026
02 Design: AI Adoption, Procurement and Governance (AU0010)

Build the governance and risk foundations for responsible AI use. Design accountability frameworks and approval pipelines, embed AI risk into enterprise governance, and establish assurance, audit readiness and incident response processes.

Duration: 30 hours Topics: 5 Funding: Levy eligible Start: June 2026
03 Implement: AI Delivery and Organisational Transformation (AU0011)

Lead AI-enabled change and represent your organisation with confidence. Drive cross-functional transformation, assess workforce impacts, support upskilling, and engage credibly with regulators, partners and the public.

Duration: 30 hours Topics: 5 Funding: Levy eligible Start: July 2026

Eligibility & funding

Eligibility, in plain English.

Learner criteria

  • Lives in EnglandLearner must live in England and spend at least 50% of their working time there.
  • Leadership roleMiddle manager leading a team, or C-suite / senior leader equivalent.
  • New content to the roleLess than 50% of the unit content already covered in the learner's current role or past training.
  • Passes admission interviewIndividual assessment — we evaluate fit to protect completion and outcome rates.

Funding route

  • Levy-paying employersFund units through existing Apprenticeship Service account. No additional budget required.
  • Inside working hoursGovernment rules require synchronous learning in working hours — we designed the delivery around that constraint.

The delivery model that survives a senior leader's calendar.

Cohort sprints with real deadlines. 1-on-1 feedback from active AI practitioners at Meta, Google, Spotify, Vinted, and elsewhere. The Protégé Effect — reviewing junior work cements the material.
